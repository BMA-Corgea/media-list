# T-8 — Spin the wheel

**Type** feature · **Risk** medium · **Intent** T-1 · **After** T-4

Evan asked for this by name and asked for the animation to be good. **The motion quality is
the requirement, not polish to be cut if time runs short.**

## Acceptance criteria

1. **AC1 — it is a wheel and it spins.** Segments for the eligible titles, a fixed pointer,
   and a spin that starts fast and decelerates over a long tail — not a linear rotation and
   not a snap.
2. **AC2 — the landing is honest.** The winner is chosen by the RNG *first*; the rotation is
   then computed to land on it. Proven by distribution: **10,000 picks across N titles must
   be uniform** (max deviation from expected under 5%), and the pointer must land inside the
   winner's segment every time.
3. **AC3 — the reveal uses the art.** After landing, the chosen title appears with its cover,
   kind, year and `why`, with a way to open its page and a way to spin again.
4. **AC4 — scoped by kind.** The same filter chips as the queue; spinning respects them.
5. **AC5 — small lists degrade correctly.** 2 titles works. 1 title: no spin theatre for a
   foregone conclusion — say so and reveal it. 0 titles: an empty state.
6. **AC6 — reduced motion.** With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, no spin: an **instant,
   equally honest** pick and reveal.
7. **AC7 — you cannot spin twice at once.** The control is disabled while spinning.
8. **AC8 — skin-driven.** No colour literals; segment tints come from tokens.

## Out of scope
Weighting by anything (position, age, rating) — the pick is uniform, deliberately.
