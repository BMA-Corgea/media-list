# T-5 — The wall: a rotating cover-art carousel

**Type** feature · **Risk** medium · **Intent** T-1 · **After** T-4

T-1's objective in one screen. *"The spreadsheet is not failing at storage, it is failing at
retrieval and desire."* This is the desire half. A carousel that is unpleasant to use fails
this ticket even if every datum on it is correct.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **AC1 — it rotates by drag.** Pointer down on the carousel and move: the cards follow the
   cursor in real time, with momentum on release and a settle onto the nearest card. Works
   with mouse and touch (Pointer Events, so one code path).
2. **AC2 — it rotates by buttons.** A control on each side steps exactly **one** card.
   Repeated clicks do not compound into a spin.
3. **AC3 — keyboard.** ← and → step one card; the focused card is the selected one; Enter
   opens it. Focus is visible.
4. **AC4 — clicking a card opens its title page** (`#/title/{id}`, T-6's route). A **drag
   must not fire a click** — releasing after movement past a small threshold selects, it does
   not navigate.
5. **AC5 — it holds up at every size of list.** 0 titles → a real empty state that invites
   adding. 1 title → no broken geometry, no infinite loop of one card. 200 titles → still
   smooth, and the DOM does not hold 200 rendered cards.
6. **AC6 — reduced motion is respected.** With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, transitions
   become instant; nothing animates, everything still works.
7. **AC7 — fully skin-driven.** No colour literal anywhere in the carousel's CSS; it reads
   only tokens from `base.css`, and looks correct in `system`, `nocturne` and `paperback`.
8. **AC8 — the current card is legible.** Title, year, kind and (if present) `why` are shown
   for the centre card, over a scrim so they survive a bright poster.

## Out of scope

Reordering (T-7), the wheel (T-8), the title page itself (T-6 — this ticket only routes to
it), the Seen archive (T-9), Pexels chrome behind it (T-11).
