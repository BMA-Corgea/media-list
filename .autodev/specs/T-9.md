# T-9 — Review and the Seen archive

**Type** feature · **Risk** medium · **Intent** T-1 · **After** T-6

T-1: *"Watching something means giving it 1–5 stars; it then moves into a browsable Seen
archive that stays worth visiting."* The archive is a trophy case, not a graveyard.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **AC1 — rating from the title page.** The button T-6 left disabled becomes live and asks
   for **1–5 stars (required)** and an optional free-text review.
2. **AC2 — the verb is right.** A game is *played*; it reads from `frontend/src/kinds.js`
   (T-6's single source) rather than re-deciding.
3. **AC3 — it leaves the queue without being deleted.** `status` → `seen`, `watched_at`
   stamped, `queue_position` cleared. It disappears from the wall, the queue and the wheel,
   and its row still exists.
4. **AC4 — the archive is browsable.** `#/seen`: cover art, stars, date, review, kind chip.
   Sortable by **date** and by **rating**; filterable by kind.
5. **AC5 — un-watching returns it.** Back to `queued`, at the end of the queue, **keeping its
   stars and review** so a re-watch does not erase the first opinion.
6. **AC6 — stars are validated.** The API refuses 0, 6 and non-integers; the schema's CHECK is
   the backstop.
7. **AC7 — the review is editable afterwards** and survives a reload.
8. **AC8 — empty archive** reads as an invitation, not an error.
9. **AC9 — skin-driven**, no colour literals.

## Out of scope
CSV (T-10), Pexels chrome (T-11).
