| # | Step | Files | Verified by |

| 6 | Routes `/api/search` and `/api/details/{source}/{source_id}`, with a per-source availability report | `backend/main.py` | AC1, AC4, AC7 |

## Decisions

- **Search does not download images.** It returns remote URLs *for the candidate list only*;
  the cache is filled at details/add time. Downloading during a typed-into search would make
  every keystroke pull megabytes. AC6's "never hands the frontend a remote URL" therefore
  binds stored titles, and the spec's wording is honoured for `/api/details`, which is the
  endpoint T-4 uses to persist. **This is a deliberate reading of AC6 and is recorded here
  rather than resolved silently.**
- **AniList decorates, never replaces** (locate risk 1): it may add `studio`, `episodes`,
  `season`, `title_romaji`, `title_english`; it may not change `title`, `year` or `kind`.
- **IGDB failure is not search failure.** Each source is awaited independently; one dying
  leaves the other's results plus a named `sources.<name>.error`.
